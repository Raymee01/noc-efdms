# Brand assets

Original official assets, retrieved 25 October 2026 at the user's request:

- Nigeria Olympic Committee: https://nigeriaolympic.org/wp-content/uploads/logod-270x300-1.png (from https://nigeriaolympic.org/)
- Commonwealth Sport logo: https://www.commonwealthsport.com/resources/cgf/v1.54.1/i/elements/cws-logo-vertical.svg
- Commonwealth Sport celebration mark: https://www.commonwealthsport.com/resources/cgf/v1.54.1/i/elements/celebration-mark-colors.svg
- Official favicon source: https://www.commonwealthsport.com/resources/cgf/v1.54.1/i/elements/cgf/favicon-32x32.png
